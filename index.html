Mobile RFID scanning ladder with a Zebra FX9600-8 and eight UHF antennas

A concept model built from your GA drawing. The 6 m aluminium mast stands on a castored trolley. The reader sits inside the 500×400×240 panel and each of its 8 ports feeds one antenna on the mast, so one pass along an aisle reads every rack level.
